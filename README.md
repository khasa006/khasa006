# 👋 Hi, I'm Tanvir!

I'm a **Senior Associate Data Scientist in Pharmacometrics at Alnylam Pharmaceuticals**, with a PhD in Public Health (Biostatistics & Data Analytics) from Florida International University.

My work spans **biostatistics, statistical genetics, Bayesian modeling, causal inference, and clinical data science**, with experience in academic research and the pharmaceutical industry.

Previously, I was a **Postdoctoral Associate in the Department of Psychiatry at Yale School of Medicine**, where I conducted genetically informed research on substance use disorders (SUDs), psychiatric conditions, and related health outcomes using large-scale biobanks and genome-wide association data.

My research interests center on applying statistical methodology, computational tools, and reproducible analytical workflows to biomedical and clinical research questions.

## 🔬 Research Interests

- 🧬 **Statistical Genetics & Epidemiology:** GWAS, PheWAS, genetic correlation, Mendelian randomization, and genetically informed studies of complex diseases.
- 📊 **Biostatistics & Bayesian Modeling:** Bayesian kernel machine regression (BKMR), hierarchical modeling, Monte Carlo simulation, and statistical methodology development.
- 💊 **Clinical Pharmacology & Pharmacometrics:** Population PK/PD modeling, simulation, clinical data analysis, and quantitative drug development.
- 🧠 **Psychiatric Epidemiology & Causal Inference:** Substance use disorders, psychiatric traits, causal inference, and large-scale biomedical datasets.
- 💻 **Statistical Computing & Reproducible Research:** R package development, analytical pipelines, simulation frameworks, and automated scientific reporting.
